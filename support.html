Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, enter your email address and tap Forgot Password?. We’ll email you a link. Open it on your phone or computer, choose a new password, then log in to the app. If you don’t see the email, check your spam folder.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Chicken Expert and choose Cancel Subscription. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

I subscribed on another device. How do I restore my purchase?

Log in with the same account, open the subscription screen, and tap Restore purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are billed by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at chickenexpertapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We’ll delete your account and uploaded photos within 30 days. Tracker data and chat history are stored only on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

How do I get the best results from a health diagnosis?

  • Use bright, even light and keep the photo in focus.
  • Center the chicken (or the egg) and show the area that concerns you, such as the comb, feathers, eyes, feet, or shell.
  • Describe what you’re seeing in the notes: when it started, appetite, droppings, behavior, and any changes in egg laying.

Is Chicken Expert a veterinarian?

No. Chicken Expert gives general information using artificial intelligence and can’t diagnose your chickens. For a seriously sick or injured bird, or if you suspect a contagious disease, contact a veterinarian or your local animal health authority.

Where is my data stored?

Your egg and flock tracker data and chat history are stored on your phone. Your account and any photos you send for diagnosis are stored securely with our service providers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Something isn’t working. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app and checking your internet connection. If the problem continues, email us with details and we’ll look into it.
