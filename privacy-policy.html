Chicken Expert

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Chicken Expert (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an iPhone app that helps backyard chicken keepers check on their flock’s health, learn about egg and coop care, ask questions, and track eggs and chickens. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

The short version: We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Your Egg & Flock Tracker data and chat history are kept on your phone.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored in a protected (hashed) form; we cannot see your password.

Photos and notes for health diagnosis. When you use Chicken Health Diagnosis or Egg Health Diagnosis, the photo you take or choose and the notes you type are uploaded so they can be analyzed. We only access your camera or photo library when you choose to take or select a photo, and only after you grant permission in iOS.

Chat messages. Messages you send to Chicken Expert in the chat, along with recent messages from the same conversation, are sent to our AI service to generate a reply.

Subscription information. If you subscribe, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions, so we and RevenueCat receive information such as your subscription status, purchase history, and an account identifier. We never see your payment card details.

Information stored only on your device. Your saved chat history, your Egg & Flock Tracker data (egg counts, notes, and chicken profiles), your onboarding answers, and app preferences are stored locally on your phone. We do not receive a copy of this information.

Technical information. Like most online services, the providers that host our service may automatically log technical data such as IP address, device type, and request times to keep the service secure and working. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics tools in the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in or reset your password.
  • To analyze your photos and notes and show health and egg guidance.
  • To answer your questions in chat.
  • To manage your subscription and restore purchases.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent abuse, fix bugs, and improve the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Supabase – provides our account system, database, file storage, and the server functions that power the app.
  • OpenAI – our AI provider. Your chat messages, photos, and notes are sent to OpenAI to generate responses. Under OpenAI’s business/API terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default.
  • RevenueCat – manages subscription status and purchases.
  • Apple – processes payments and subscriptions through the App Store.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users’ safety, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

4. AI-generated results

Health and egg results and chat answers are generated by artificial intelligence from the photos, notes, and messages you provide. They are general information for backyard chicken keepers, are not veterinary advice, and may be incomplete or wrong. See our Terms of Service.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information and any photos you uploaded for as long as your account is active, so the app can keep working for you. You can ask us to delete your account and the data we hold about you at any time by emailing chickenexpertapp@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except for information we must keep to meet legal obligations. Information stored only on your phone (such as tracker data and chat history) is removed when you delete the app or clear the data in the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn camera and photo library access on or off at any time in iOS Settings → Chicken Expert.
  • You can ask us for a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.
  • You can cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have additional rights such as objecting to or restricting certain processing, and you may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our servers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private.

8. Children’s privacy

Chicken Expert is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process and store information in countries other than your own, including the United States. By using the app you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries, which may have different data protection rules than your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above, and for significant changes we will provide a notice in the app or by other appropriate means. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your data? Email us at chickenexpertapp@gmail.com.
